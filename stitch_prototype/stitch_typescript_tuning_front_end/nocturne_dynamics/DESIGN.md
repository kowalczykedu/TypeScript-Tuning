## Brand & Style

This design system translates the underground, high-precision world of nocturnal automotive performance into a tailored, minimalist academic suite. It steers deliberately clear of cluttered race-telemetry gauges, excessive skeuomorphism, and overwhelming diagnostic readouts. Instead, it positions tuning as an art of measured control: pristine sheet metal under dim workshop lighting, cold garage asphalt, and razor-sharp execution.

The style combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Cyber-Industrial** and **Glassmorphism** accents:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep-night garage vibe achieved through pitch-black foundations, monolithic surfaces, and restrained luminous accents.
- **Emotion:** Surgical, confident, elite, and focused. The UI induces a sense of absolute mechanical precision and composure.
- **Audience:** Automotive engineering students, technical enthusiasts, and high-end software calibrators who prioritize clarity, spatial luxury, and decisive aesthetic execution over chaotic data streams.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure obsidian foundations, deep graphite chassis, and controlled neon releases. Color is used strictly for hierarchy, state guidance, and surgical focus.

### Base & Chassis Tones
- **Pitch Obsidian Canvas (`#08070B`):** The absolute background anchor, evoking an unlit dyno bay.
- **Deep Graphite Base (`#0F0E17`):** Primary card, panel, and sheet surfaces.
- **Elevated Graphite (`#181622`):** Hovered states, floating toolbars, and nested modules.
- **Border / Stroke Graphite (`#272338`):** Low-contrast structural boundaries preserving edge clarity.

### Accent Accords
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact structural headlines, critical values, and focused states.
- **Hyper Violet (`#9E00FF`):** Primary interaction cues, active toggles, and key stage indicators.
- **Containment Magenta (`#D946EF`):** Expressive secondary highlights, glow gradients, and peak thresholds.
- **Deep Velvet Violet (`#7928CA`):** Ambient backdrop glows, inactive pill backgrounds, and subtle badges.
- **Muted Steel (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary metadata, subheadings, and baseline metrics.
- **Dim Industrial Slate (`#64748B`):** Inactive icons, field placeholders, and auxiliary captions.

### Strict Functional States
- **Valid / Operational Green (`#10B981`):** Strictly reserved for successful flashings, verified stage configurations, and optimal mechanical tolerances. Never used for decorative accents.
- **Critical / Blown Red (`#EF4444`):** Strictly reserved for unstable ECU states, redlines, component failure warnings, and invalid balance maps.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-end structural automotive branding with rigorous mechanical verification:
- **Headline Font (`Syne`):** Imparts an aggressive, sculpted, wide-track posture. Headings evoke carved aerodynamic body lines.
- **Body Font (`Inter`):** Delivers clean readability for conceptual lessons, mechanical parameters, and narrative feedback.
- **Technical & Metric Font (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to telemetry values, dyno stats, stage markers, and code-level calibration tags. Its monospace nature prevents layout shift during real-time value changes.

### Typographic Rules
- All `label-*` tokens must be styled in uppercase when serving as mechanical parameter identifiers (e.g., `BOOST_MAP`, `STAGE_02`, `AFR_RATIO`).
- Headings must always default to pure `#FFFFFF` to punch forward against the dark background.
- Explanatory body text stays strictly on `#94A3B8` to prevent visual fatigue and maintain dark-room optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined **Fluid Grid** with generous atmospheric breathing space. Instead of cramming data like a traditional racing cockpit, it adopts a gallery-grade presentation: generous negative space around high-impact car modules.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width capped at `1440px` centered.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex dual-column parameter editors drop into staggered horizontal segments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Cards stretch edge-to-edge within safe margins; horizontal swipe strips replace dense grids.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Card interiors must default to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) padding to preserve an expansive, airy feel.
- Component internal stacks use `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) to keep associated mechanical labels and metrics tightly bound.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Stacking** paired with **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders** and **Subtle Violet Photonic Glows**.

### Surface Layers
- **Ground Floor (`#08070B`):** Canvas backdrop.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#0F0E17`):** Primary structural panels and resting card tiles. Outlined by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 Floating (`#181622`):** Active selections, flyout menus, and modal dialogs. Outlined by `1px solid rgba(158, 0, 255, 0.25)`.

### Ambient Radiance & Shadow Rules
- **Resting:** No heavy drop shadows. Rely entirely on the edge border and the background contrast.
- **Active / Stage Ready:** An electric violet back-illumination: `0px 8px 32px -4px rgba(158, 0, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Hazard / Blown State:** A low, focused crimson back-glow: `0px 8px 32px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.22)`.
- **Surface Frost:** Dialog overlays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(8, 7, 11, 0.75)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft / Precision Engineering** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Automotive tuning requires high structural integrity—overly rounded bubbles or circular capsules detract from mechanical credibility, while raw 0px edges feel unfinished.

### Border Radius Tokens
- **Standard (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Checkboxes, code chips, parameter pills, and input elements.
- **Large (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Component cards, modal surfaces, dyno stat containers, and visual display previews.
- **Extra Large (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Primary viewport hero containers and full-canvas drawer overlays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Flash / Commit / Run):** Gradient fill transitioning from `#7928CA` to `#9E00FF`. Text is `#FFFFFF`, `Syne` weight 700. On hover, the container brightens with a subtle ambient bloom (`0 0 20px rgba(158, 0, 255, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Tweak / Inspect):** Surface `#181622` with a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border. Text is `#94A3B8`, shifting to `#FFFFFF` with border `#9E00FF` on hover.
- **Critical (Abort / Rollback):** Ghost style with `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`. Text is `#EF4444`. On active focus, floods background to `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`.

### Cards & ECU Modules
- Background `#0F0E17`, 8px roundedness, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Generous internal padding (`1.5rem`).
- Header displays category indicator in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-sm`, color `#64748B`), paired with the unit title in `Syne` (`headline-sm`, `#FFFFFF`).

### Chips & Parameter Badges
- Background `#181622`, border `1px solid rgba(158, 0, 255, 0.2)`.
- Typographic style is strictly `label-md` with `JetBrains Mono`.
- Active stage chips feature a 6px glowing indicator dot (`#9E00FF` or `#D946EF`).

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Background `#08070B` (inset feel against the `#0F0E17` card).
- Border `1px solid #272338`, radius 4px.
- Text uses `JetBrains Mono` (`#FFFFFF`). Placeholder uses `#64748B`.
- Focused state transforms the border to `#9E00FF` with an inner violet sheen.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes: 18x18px, dark inset box with a sharp mechanical tick. Checked state fills `#9E00FF`.
- Toggles: Track is `#181622` (`1px solid #272338`). Thumb is a crisp 4px-rounded square sliding into `#D946EF` glow on active engagement.

### Lists & Dyno Stage Rows
- Rows are delineated not by loud gridlines, but by subtle hover shifts from `#0F0E17` to `#181622`.
- Left-side indicator bar (2px wide) activates in `#9E00FF` only on cursor hover or persistent selection.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Stage Slider:** A custom track bar showing Stage 1, Stage 2, and Stage 3 presets with neon notch points, eliminating cluttered multi-input mapping.
- **Tuning State Bar:** Top-level notification banner utilizing `#10B981` (Validated Map) or `#EF4444` (Engine Parameter Conflict) strictly across a 1px top stroke.
